Tether

Effective 18 September 2026

Privacy Policy

Tether does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics.

What the app stores

Tether saves a small amount of data on your device so the game can remember where you left off:

  • your best score and your last twelve run scores
  • lifetime totals — runs played, catches, time in flight
  • your results on daily runs, for the last fourteen days
  • your settings — haptics, sound, music, reduced motion, high contrast, catch rings

This is written to your device's own app storage. It never leaves your device, it is not readable by other apps, and it is not sent anywhere. Deleting Tether deletes all of it. You can also clear it from inside the app at any time, under Settings → Reset Best.

What the app does not do

  • No network. Tether contains no networking code. It does not connect to the internet, and it works identically in airplane mode.
  • No tracking. Tether does not use the advertising identifier, does not track you across apps or websites, and contains no analytics or attribution software.
  • No advertising. Tether shows no ads and contains no advertising software.
  • No third-party code. Tether has no third-party SDKs or dependencies of any kind.
  • No data sold or shared. There is nothing to sell or share.

Children

Tether is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13. Because no information is collected, none can be disclosed.

Changes

If a future version of Tether adds a feature that changes any of this — online leaderboards, for example — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change with it.
